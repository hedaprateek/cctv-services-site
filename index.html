Around the installation

The wiring work most camera fitters leave for someone else.

A camera job is mostly an electrical job. Cable has to get from the corner of a terrace to a recorder two floors down, on a supply that doesn't drop out, through conduit that survives the monsoon. All of that is done here too.

  • 01Concealed conduit & casing-capping runs Chased into the wall on new construction, surface casing on finished buildings — not cable stapled along a skirting.
  • 02Dedicated power point and MCB for the recorder So the DVR isn't sharing a socket with a fridge or a welding point.
  • 03Earthing and surge protection for outdoor cameras The single most common reason outdoor cameras die in the first season.
  • 04UPS or inverter backup on the recorder Recording survives a power cut — which is exactly when it tends to matter.
  • 05Poles, brackets, junction boxes, weatherproof glanding Fabricated and fitted for gates, boundary walls and open compounds.
  • 06Cable routing across floors, terraces and boundary walls Including core drilling, terrace crossings and runs to a separate guard room.
  • 07General electrical repairs on the same visit Loose points, dead sockets and old wiring found while the job is open.

Why it matters who does the wiring

When the cameras and the electrical are split between two people, every later fault turns into an argument about whose side it is. Doing both means one number to call, one person accountable, and no waiting a week for an electrician to come back and finish a job that's already half open.

It also means the survey accounts for supply and cable routing from the start — so the quote doesn't grow halfway through the installation.

How a job runs

Survey first. Quote second. No surprises third.

  1. 01 — Survey

    Walk the site

    Camera positions marked on the spot, existing wiring and supply checked, blind spots identified. Usually half an hour, and there's no charge for it.

  2. 02 — Quote

    Written, itemised

    Camera by camera: brand, model, resolution, cable, recorder, storage and labour listed separately, so you can see exactly what you're paying for.

  3. 03 — Install

    Cabling, then cameras

    All the cable runs and power work first, then mounting, then the recorder. Most residential and shop jobs finish the same day.

  4. 04 — Handover

    Set up on your phone

    App installed and tested on your device, playback and clip export demonstrated, and angles re-checked once you've seen a night's footage.

Afterwards Annual maintenance contracts for shops, offices and societies — periodic cleaning, angle correction, drive health checks and priority fault visits.
