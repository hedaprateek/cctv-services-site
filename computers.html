Peripherals

Printers, scanners and everything plugged in.

  • Inkjet and laser printer servicing Head cleaning, cartridge refill, paper feed problems and print quality faults.

Annual maintenance for offices

For shops and offices running a few machines, a quarterly contract covers every computer and printer on site, with call-outs included rather than charged each time something stops working.

Machines are cleaned, checked and updated on a schedule, so the faults that would have stopped work on a busy day get caught on a quiet one instead.
