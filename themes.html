Each block below is the real thing — header bar, hero band, buttons, a service card and the chip row — rendered in a different warm palette. Number 2 is what's live right now (number 2). A few things worth knowing before you pick

Every one of these is checked so the text passes contrast on its own background — which is why most call buttons carry dark text rather than white. Getting that wrong is the quickest way to lose the one click that matters on a trades site.

  • Green stays green. The only cool colour left is the status dot for a healthy hard disk — it means "fine", so it shouldn't be warm.
  • The CCTV animations don't change. The camera wall cycles channels, a motion box locks on, and the camera on the CCTV page sweeps its arc, whichever palette you pick.
  • Mixing is fine — say "04 but with the marigold call button" and that works.
  • Nothing else changes. The pages, the copy and the requirement builder stay exactly as they are; only the token block at the top of assets/site.css gets swapped.
