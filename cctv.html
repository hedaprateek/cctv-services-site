How a job runs

Survey first. Quote second. No surprises third.

  1. 1

    Walk the site

    Camera positions marked on the spot, existing wiring and supply checked, blind spots identified. Usually half an hour, and no charge for it.

  2. 2

    Written, itemised quote

    Camera by camera: brand, model, resolution, cable, recorder, storage and labour listed separately, so you can see what you're paying for.

  3. 3

    Cabling, then cameras

    All cable runs and power work first, then mounting, then the recorder. Most homes and shops finish the same day.

  4. 4

    Handover

    App installed on your phone and tested, playback demonstrated, angles re-checked once you've seen a night's footage.

Afterwards: annual maintenance contracts for shops, offices and societies — periodic cleaning, angle correction, drive health checks and priority fault visits.
